Download ThatSoundLikeMe

ThatSoundLikeMe lets you imitate a sound with your voice and search for similar sounds on Freesound. The microphone recording and embedding model run locally; see the privacy policy for details.

Desktop releases for macOS and Windows, including checksums and SBOMs, are published on the GitHub releases page and made available through MuseHub.

Installation and removal

On Windows, run the downloaded installer and choose the standard per-user installation. Use Windows Settings → Apps to uninstall. On macOS, open the signed and notarized application ZIP, move ThatSoundLikeMe to Applications, and delete it from Applications to uninstall.
