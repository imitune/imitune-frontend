Privacy policy

Last updated: 31 July 2026.

How search works

When you choose to record, your microphone audio and the ONNX model run locally in your browser or desktop application. The recording is converted locally into a 960-value embedding. A search sends that embedding, not the raw recording, to the ThatSoundLikeMe API. The API queries Pinecone and returns Freesound links.

Optional research feedback

Nothing is stored for research unless you explicitly enable data sharing and rate results. When enabled, your recording, ratings, result context and timestamp are sent to the ThatSoundLikeMe API and stored in Vercel Blob for research. You can turn data sharing off at any time; it affects future submissions.

Service providers

Search uses Pinecone. Rate limiting uses Upstash. Optional feedback storage uses Vercel Blob. Results are supplied as links to Freesound. Their terms and privacy policies apply when you use their services.

No background telemetry

ThatSoundLikeMe does not send analytics, microphone recordings, or other information in the background. Network transfers happen only when you request a search or, separately, opt in to research feedback.
